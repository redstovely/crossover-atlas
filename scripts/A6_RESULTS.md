**Conjecture tested** (RECONCILIATION T4 / thesis 4.5.4 + splitting rule):
for omega not in {0, 180} and e below the validity bound, the O(e^2) model
has exactly **m** critical inclinations in (0, 90]. Circular baseline
j = ceil(m/2); doubling of all criticals except the equatorial (odd-odd)
or polar i*=90 (k even) singles gives 2j-1 = m (m odd) or 2j = m (m even).

**Grid**: all 127 coprime (k,m), 1 <= m < k <= 20; e in {0.02, 0.05, 0.10,
0.15}; omega in {15, 30, ..., 165} deg (11 values); validity gate
e < e_max(k,m) = (-2 + sqrt(4 + 6(k-m)/m))/3 (inventory (4.82) exact).
Method: global (u2, r) tangency scan of the corrected system (4.56)+(4.55),
2^16-point grid + bisection — no continuation/ancestry assumption, so
criticals born at finite e are detected (and are, beyond e_max; see below).

## Counterexample check (within validity)

**None.** Every configuration with e < e_max(k,m) has exactly m distinct
critical inclinations in (0, 90].

## omega-independence of the count

Within validity: the count is identical across all 11 omega values for
every (k, m, e) — as the parity/counting propositions require.

Set-level parity: criticals(omega) vs criticals(180-omega) agree pairwise
to 6.5e-13 deg across the whole sweep. (The O(e^2) tangency system depends on omega only through sin(omega)
and cos(2*omega), so omega -> 180-omega is an exact symmetry; the sweep's
omega > 90 half is a consistency replica.)

Beyond validity, 57 (k,m,e) combos have omega-dependent
counts — expected: mechanism-1 births are omega-dependent (worst near
omega = 90, cf. (4.80)).

## Verdict

The sweep supports upgrading the eccentric count from bare Conjecture to
**"Conjecture with exhaustive numerical verification on (k <= 20)"**:
zero counterexamples in 4895 in-scope configurations, count
omega-independent, polar persistence and parity exactly as the splitting
rule (E4.34) predicts, and every deviation beyond e_max(k,m) has the
mechanism-1 signature the theory itself predicts (low-inclination births,
omega-dependent, worst near omega = 90). This is evidence, not proof: the
paper should state the result as a Conjecture/Numerically-verified
Proposition with hypotheses e < e_max(k,m), omega not in {0, 180}, and
cite this sweep; the analytic gaps (exhaustiveness lemma, uniform e-bar
bound, i-sweep monotonicity — inventory sec. 9 items 1-6) remain what
separates it from a Theorem.
